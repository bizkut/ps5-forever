Fix
  PS5-title-only contiguous-run batching of the temporary PROT_NONE operation
  after direct-unit mapping/zeroing. Each unit remains separately allocated,
  owned and rollback-tracked. Existing committed units split runs and are never
  reclosed or zeroed. New mappings remain non-executable throughout preparation.
  C:\wineps5-tune.txt: direct_commit_batch=off restores per-unit calls;
  direct_commit_batch=on enables batching (default), reread at most once/second.
  No IOCP or message-queue semantics were changed.
  Existing CPU sampler was shared with maintenance/PsmKit children; profiling
  now records per-request client histograms and bounded server queue timing.
